Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Waslagoo ("we," "us," "our") operates waslagoo.com. This page explains what data we collect when you visit, why, and what rights you have over it.

1. Who we are

Waslagoo is an independent affiliate website that helps travellers compare and find eSIM data plans for Morocco. We do not sell eSIMs directly — purchases are completed on Airalo's own website, and we may earn a commission when you buy through a link on our site.

Contact: hello@waslagoo.com

2. What data we collect

We collect very little, because the site itself doesn't require an account, a form, or a purchase to be completed on it.

  • Language and currency preference — stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so the site remembers your FR/EN and EUR/GBP choice. This never leaves your device or gets sent to us.
  • Standard technical data — like most websites, our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare) automatically processes technical information such as your IP address and browser type, to serve the page and protect the site from bots and abuse. We don't access this as personal profiling data ourselves.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or trackers. Waslagoo does not run ads and does not sell or share data with advertisers.

3. When you click through to Airalo

When you click a "See plans" or "Activate" button, you leave waslagoo.com and go to Airalo's website to browse or buy. Any data you provide there — payment details, email, name — is collected and handled by Airalo under their own privacy policy, not ours. We encourage you to review it before purchasing.

4. Legal basis for processing (UK/EU visitors)

Where UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies to you, our legal basis for the limited technical processing described above is legitimate interest — specifically, keeping the site secure and functioning correctly. We do not process special category data, and we do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

5. Your rights

Since we don't hold accounts or personal profiles on our visitors, there's generally very little personal data of yours for us to access, correct, or delete. If you believe we hold any information about you and want it reviewed or removed, contact us at the email above and we'll respond promptly. UK and EU residents also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO; in the EU, your national supervisory authority).

6. Children's privacy

Waslagoo is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the site or our partnerships change. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

This policy describes waslagoo.com as it currently operates. If Waslagoo begins processing payments, accounts, or additional personal data directly in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect that, and reviewed by a qualified professional as appropriate.
