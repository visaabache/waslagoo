Family holiday in Agadir: the phone data plan that avoids bill shock

The real risk on a family holiday is not your own phone. It is four devices, all quietly updating, streaming and syncing on a roaming connection. Here is how to set up the family's data once, before you fly.

Where bill shock really comes from

  • Kids' tablets streaming video on a mobile connection.
  • App and system updates running in the background.
  • Cloud photo backups uploading every holiday snap.
  • Podcasts and games downloading on the move.
  • Several devices each connecting to a roaming network at once.

None of it feels like heavy use in the moment, which is exactly why it adds up.

The five-minute lockdown (do it at home)

  1. Turn data roaming off on every UK line, including the children's devices.
  2. Set app updates, iCloud or Google Photos backups and podcast downloads to Wi‑Fi only.
  3. Switch off cellular data for streaming and gaming apps on kids' devices, using Screen Time or Family Link.
  4. Download films, shows, games and audiobooks before you fly.
  5. If your phone allows it, set a data warning on the eSIM line.

When unlimited makes sense

If you would rather not think about data at all and expect to hotspot for streaming, an unlimited plan removes the counting. Read the fair-use terms first, as some unlimited plans slow speeds after heavy use.

Half-term, Easter and summer

School-holiday flights arrive in waves, and the airport gets busy. That is a strong argument for being online before you reach the kiosk queue. If you are pre-booking a transfer, ask about child seats at the same time. The full arrival sequence is in our Agadir airport eSIM guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can one eSIM cover a whole family?

Often it can, using your phone's hotspot, provided the plan allows tethering. It will use data faster, so choose a larger plan.

How do I stop my kids' tablets using mobile data abroad?

Turn off cellular data and roaming for those devices, use Screen Time or Family Link, and download content before you fly.
