The surf-week data budget

These are rules of thumb, not lab measurements, but they are close enough to plan with.

ActivityRough data useOver a week
WhatsApp messages and voice notesA few MB a dayUnder 0.1 GB
Maps and navigationRoughly 5 to 10 MB an hourUnder 0.1 GB

Add it up and a light week is about 2 to 3 GB, a typical week with photos and social is about 4 to 6 GB, and a content-creating week can pass 10 GB.

What about camp and guesthouse Wi‑Fi?

Many surf camps and guesthouses have Wi‑Fi, but speed and reliability vary, especially when everyone uploads at once in the evening. Treat it as a bonus for big uploads, and keep your eSIM for maps, forecasts, messages and everything on the move.

Ten ways to make your data last

  1. Download offline maps of the coast road and your village before you fly.
  2. Turn off automatic photo and video upload over mobile data.
  3. Switch off video autoplay in social apps.
  4. Use your phone's low data mode.
  5. Download playlists at home or on Wi‑Fi.
  6. Upload your session footage on camp Wi‑Fi, not mobile data.
  7. Update apps and your phone before you leave the UK.
  8. Use low-data settings for calls in your messaging app.
  9. Turn off background app refresh.
  10. Check your usage screen midweek so there are no surprises.

Getting from Agadir airport to Taghazout

Taghazout is about 40 km from the airport, roughly 45 to 60 minutes by road. Recent traveller reports suggest a grand taxi costs around 250 to 350 MAD for the whole car, more late at night, so agree the fare first. Many flights land late, so pre-booking a transfer is worth considering. Our landing guide covers the airport end in detail.

Frequently asked questions

Is 5 GB enough for a week in Taghazout?

For most people, yes, if you use maps, messaging and some social media. Heavy uploaders and video callers may want 10 GB.

Can I rely on surf camp Wi‑Fi?

Not entirely. Wi‑Fi speed and reliability vary, particularly in the evening, so keep mobile data for the essentials.
