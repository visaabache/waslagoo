Casablanca airport (CMN): the airport train, taxis and getting online

Casablanca is the one major Moroccan airport with a railway station under the terminal. Here is how the train works, what it costs, when it stops, and how to get online before you reach the platform.

The short answer

Casablanca's Mohammed V airport (CMN) is about 30 km from the city, and unlike Agadir or Marrakech it has a railway station under Terminal 1. According to the airport authority, ONDA, direct trains run to Casa-Voyageurs and Casa-Port, the trip to Casa-Voyageurs takes around 30 minutes, and a ticket costs 50 MAD in second class (about €4.70) or 70 MAD in first.

For your phone, do what works everywhere in Morocco: install an eSIM at home and switch it on after landing, so your maps, timetable and ticket are online before you reach the platform.

Your first hour at CMN

  1. Before you fly

    Install your eSIM on Wi-Fi

    Do it at home or at your departure airport, not at the gate. Our step-by-step install guide covers iPhone and Android. Download an offline map of Casablanca and screenshot your hotel's address.

  2. Wheels down

    Switch flight mode off

    Wait a minute for your phone to find a Moroccan network, then message your hotel or host with your arrival time.

  3. Passport control and baggage

    Use the queue

    Immigration queues depend on how many flights have landed together. Check your train time while you wait, and have a little cash for the taxi or small purchases.

  4. Arrivals

    Follow the signs to the ONCF station

    ONDA says the station is in the basement of Terminal 1, with direct access to the airport concourse. If you are not sure where it is, ask at the information desk or check the signs for "Gare ONCF".

  5. On the platform

    Pick the right station

    Casa-Voyageurs is the main station for onward trains. Casa-Port is closer to the waterfront. Ask your hotel which one is nearer to you before you travel.

What about a taxi?

The train is the only airport-to-city fare we found published by an official body. Taxi prices are a different story: traveller guides report anything from roughly 250 to 400 MAD (about £20 to £32) for central Casablanca, which is a wide range. If you take a taxi, agree the price before your bags go in the boot, and ask whether it is for the whole car.

If you land after the last train, or you are carrying a lot of luggage, a pre-booked transfer at a fixed price removes the haggling. Decide before you fly, not at midnight.

Heading beyond Casablanca

Casa-Voyageurs is the main intercity hub. From there, regular ONCF trains run to Marrakech (guides put the journey at about three hours), Rabat, Fès and elsewhere, and the Al Boraq high-speed line serves Rabat and Tangier. The airport train does not run to Marrakech directly, so plan a change at Casa-Voyageurs.

Getting online

An eSIM installed before you fly means you can check the live timetable and buy a ticket online without hunting for Wi-Fi. A data-only eSIM does not give you a Moroccan phone number, so keep your UK SIM for texts and switch data roaming off on it.

You can also buy a local SIM in Morocco. We have not been able to confirm current SIM kiosk locations and opening hours at CMN, so do not count on one if you land late. If you are unsure which route suits you, read SIM card vs eSIM and the plan guide.

Four mistakes to avoid

  1. Assuming the train runs all night. Trains from the airport generally stop around 22:50, so a late landing may mean a taxi.
  2. Taking the first taxi quote. Compare it with the train fare, and agree the price first.
  3. Picking the wrong station. Casa-Voyageurs and Casa-Port are different stops. Check which one suits your hotel.
  4. Arriving with no offline map. A dead signal and an unfamiliar street are a poor combination.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a train from Casablanca airport to the city?

Yes. ONDA says the ONCF station is in the basement of Terminal 1, with direct trains to Casa-Voyageurs and Casa-Port, among other stations.

How long does the airport train take?

ONDA gives around 30 minutes to Casa-Voyageurs. Some guides quote up to 45 minutes, so allow a little extra.

How much is the airport train?

ONDA lists 50 MAD in second class and 70 MAD in first class, roughly €4.70 and €6.60. Check ONCF for current fares.

Does the train run at night?

Trains from the airport generally run from about 06:50 to 22:50, according to ONDA. After that you will need a taxi or a pre-booked transfer.

Is there a direct train from the airport to Marrakech?

No. The airport train goes to Casablanca stations. For Marrakech, change at Casa-Voyageurs onto a regular ONCF service.

How much is a taxi from Casablanca airport?

Traveller guides report roughly 250 to 400 MAD to central Casablanca, but we found no official tariff. Agree the price before you set off.
