Agadir winter sun from the UK: your pre-flight phone checklist

Agadir is one of the easiest winter-sun escapes from the UK: a direct flight of under four hours. Here is the phone checklist to run through before you go, so your holiday isn't spent hunting for signal.

Two weeks before

  • Check your phone supports eSIM. Dial *#06#. If an EID number appears, it does.
  • Check it is unlocked. If it is network-locked, ask your provider; UK providers are required to unlock on request.
  • Update your phone's software and your key apps while you are on home Wi‑Fi.

The week before

  • Buy and install your Morocco eSIM on Wi‑Fi, and label the line "Morocco".
  • Switch data roaming off on your UK line, and choose the Morocco line for mobile data.
  • Download offline maps of Agadir and the route from the airport to your hotel.
  • Screenshot your hotel or riad address and save your driver's or host's WhatsApp number.
  • Download boarding passes and any films or podcasts for the journey.
  • Back up your photos so there is space for the holiday.

On the day, at the UK airport

  • Charge your phone and pack a power bank in your hand luggage.
  • Keep flight mode on for the flight.
  • Withdraw or change some cash if you can, because taxis at Agadir generally want dirhams.

On landing at Agadir

Switch flight mode off, wait for the Moroccan network, then message your driver or hotel. The full minute-by-minute sequence is in our Agadir airport eSIM guide.

While you are there

  • Use hotel Wi‑Fi for big downloads and uploads, and your eSIM for maps and messages.
  • Check your data usage screen midweek.
  • Keep your UK SIM active for text codes from banks and apps, with roaming data off.
  • Use a VPN on public Wi‑Fi if you handle anything sensitive.

Coming home

  • Delete the expired eSIM profile to keep your settings tidy.
  • Check your UK line's roaming setting is back the way you like it.
  • Keep the receipt if you need it for expenses.

When is winter-sun season?

Roughly October to April. Days in Agadir are often mild and sunny, though evenings can be cool, so pack a layer. Half-term and Christmas flights are the busiest, which makes an eSIM that is already installed even more useful.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check if my phone supports eSIM?

Dial *#06#. If an EID number appears, your phone supports eSIM. Also make sure it is network-unlocked.

Should I delete my eSIM after the trip?

You can. Once a plan has expired, deleting the profile keeps your settings tidy.
